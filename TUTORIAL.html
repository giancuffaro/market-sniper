MARKET SNIPER

One-tap 0DTE options execution for Webull · setup, keys, data, trading & futures — all in one guide
Read first. This tool places real orders with real money in LIVE mode. Running it LIVE requires a $4.99/month OPRA market-data subscription from Webull (Part 3). PAPER mode is free and simulated. Nothing here is financial advice.

New computer? Install Python 3 from python.org (tick "Add Python to PATH"), double-click INSTALL.bat, wait for VERIFIED, then START-MARKET-SNIPER.bat. Done.

Every day after that: just START-MARKET-SNIPER.bat — it unblocks any new files, restarts cleanly, and opens your browser by itself.

Part 2 — Get your API key & secret (free)

  1. Log in at developer.webull.com with your normal Webull credentials.
  2. Go to Getting Started → Individual Application, register an application (any name), enable 2FA.
  3. Once approved you get an API key and a secret — two long random strings. Copy both.
  4. One key usually covers ALL your accounts (cash, margin, futures) — the app lets you pick which one to trade at login.
Treat the key + secret like your bank login. The app keeps them on your machine only.

Part 5 — The dashboard

  • Price strip: live SPY / QQQ / SPX, refreshed every ~5s.
  • Buy buttons: show the exact strike and price one tap buys (STRIKE 756 · $1.24), refreshed every 5s. Orders are marketable limits — priced ~2% through the quote so they fill instantly like market orders (Webull forbids true market orders on options). Often you fill BETTER than quoted.
  • In a trade: big live P&L, real mark, your TP/SL levels in amber, one CLOSE button. A "FILL CONFIRMED" banner corrects your entry to the real fill within seconds.
  • Blotter: today's closed trades + day net. LOCK instantly returns to the login screen.

Part 6 — CONFIGURATION panel

  • Strike selection: 1 strike out-of-the-money (default) or 1 in-the-money. QQQ at 755 buying a call: OTM=756, ITM=754.
  • Take profit: ¢ premium move · $ total profit · next whole number (calls at 755.02 close when spot hits 756).
  • Stop loss: ¢ · $ · underlying price level (e.g. stop at 754.75 exactly).
  • Mirror trading: copy every trade to your OWN second account (LIVE).
  • Auto-lock: lock to the login screen after N idle minutes (never mid-trade).
TP/SL in LIVE fire REAL close orders automatically once enabled.

Part 7 — Trading hours

New trades: 9:30 AM – 3:40 PM ET. Closing: until 4:00 PM ET. The 3:40 cutoff is Market Sniper's own safety buffer, not a broker rule — US equity/ETF options trade until 4:00 PM ET at every broker. To trade later, edit ENTRY_CUTOFF in config.py (e.g. (15, 55)). The buffer exists because a 0DTE opened minutes before the bell leaves no room to manage it.

Part 8 — Futures (separate app)

START-FUTURES.bat opens a second app at 127.0.0.1:8010: MNQ / MES, one-tap LONG or SHORT, TP/SL in points (MNQ $2/pt, MES $5/pt), and a trailing stop. It's fully working in paper with live prices. Futures LIVE additionally needs: futures approval on your Webull account, the CME data subscription ($228/mo), and the futures order wiring — ask Claude when ready.

Part 9 — Troubleshooting

  • "Windows protected your PC" on first launch: More info → Run anyway (once). The launcher unblocks everything after that.
  • "python is not recognized": install Python from python.org with "Add to PATH", rerun INSTALL.bat.
  • 403 / subscription message on the buy buttons: the OPRA subscription (Part 3) is missing or still propagating.
  • Wrong/old version number on the connect screen: stale code or a ghost server — the launcher kills ghosts; re-copy files.
  • Anything else: run CHECK-SETUP.bat and share the output with Claude.
