FUTURES SETTINGS

★ ROUND-NUMBER ENTRY
Rests a limit on the nearest 25 — fills at that price or better, or not at all.
★ RATCHET (points)
step pts
Stop climbs a rung at a time and never drops. 12.5 → breakeven, 25 → +12.5, 37.5 → +25. No take-profit.
WHAT A TRADE COSTS YOU
Every backtest below is charged these on each trade, in and out. Put in what YOUR broker actually charges — a strategy that makes 0.2 points a trade looks profitable until you pay $1.24 plus a tick to get in and out, and then it isn't. One MNQ tick is $0.50; one MES tick is $1.25.
HISTORICAL DATA — for deep backtests (optional)
Export bars in NinjaTrader (Tools → Historical Data → Export) and upload here. Backtests then use YOUR data — real deep 1-minute history. Without a file, they fall back to free Yahoo data (limited to ~1 month of 1-min).
