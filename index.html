★ ABSOLUTE ENTRY with RATCHET TRAILING
step %
The entry. Waits for the underlying to reach the level in front of price, then buys. Levels are every whole dollar plus the X2.50 and X7.50 halves — 707.00, 707.50, 708.00 … 712.00, 712.50, 713.00. Calls wait for the level at or below price, puts at or above.
The exit. One switch, because an entry with no exit is not a system. The stop climbs and never comes back down. It always sits one step below the best you have seen:
best 0% → stop −10% · +10% → breakeven · +20% → +10% · +30% → +20% … no cap.
Percent is of the premium you paid. Reaching +10% does not close you — it moves the stop to breakeven. The trade ends when price falls back into the stop, or you press CLOSE.
Switched off, the buy buttons fire instantly at the ask and nothing manages the exit — you are closing it by hand.
STRIKE SELECTION
QQQ at 713.40 buying a CALL: ATM picks the 713, ITM1 the 713, ITM2 the 712. PUTS mirror it.
ATM takes the nearest strike but never one that is out of the money — if the nearest lands OTM it steps one in, so there is always real value in it. Deeper in the money costs more and tracks the stock more closely. Depth counts strikes, so on TSLA (2.50 steps) 2 deep is $5.00, not $2.
