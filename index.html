SETTINGS

★ MY CONFIG — round-number entry
When ON, BUY arms instead of firing: it waits for the underlying to reach the nearest whole dollar, then buys the ask. Auto-sets a +$1 take-profit (next whole number) and a 10% stop. Overrides the TP/SL below while enabled.
STRIKE SELECTION
QQQ at 755 buying a CALL: OTM picks the 756; ITM picks the 754 (costs more, already has value). PUTS mirror this.
TAKE PROFIT — closes the trade automatically
¢: entry 1.24 + 30¢ → closes 1.54. $: whole position up that much. Next whole number: calls entered with QQQ at 755.02 close when QQQ hits 756.
STOP LOSS — closes the trade automatically
Underlying price level: calls entered at 755.02 with stop 754.75 close if QQQ drops there. Puts trigger when spot rises to the level.
MIRROR TRADING — your own second account (LIVE only)
Every buy and close copies to the selected saved account at market. Only accounts that belong to YOU.
AUTO-LOCK
minutes idle
Locks to the account screen after inactivity (never while in a trade). The LOCK button locks instantly.
NEW STRATEGY
Enabled strategies run automatically while the app is open and connected. Each places ONE entry per day when its condition is met, then manages it with the take-profit and stop you set. You can enable several at once. Test in PAPER first.
