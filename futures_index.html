FUTURES SETTINGS

★ ROUND-NUMBER ENTRY
When ON, BUY / SELL rests a LIMIT order on the nearest level instead of firing at market. LONG buys the level below price, SHORT sells the level above — so the order can only fill at that price or better. No slippage. Ex: LONG MNQ at 28,716 on a 50-pt grid rests a buy limit at 28,700; SHORT there rests a sell limit at 28,750. On NinjaTrader / Tradovate / Topstep the order is really working at the broker (CANCEL on the amber bar pulls it). Trade-off vs a market order: if price never comes back to the level, you don't get filled. Your Take Profit / Stop / Trailing below still apply once it fills.
TAKE PROFIT (points)
Closes when the trade is up this many points. MNQ: 1 pt = $2/contract · MES: 1 pt = $5/contract. Ex: LONG MNQ +10 pts = +$20 per contract.
STOP LOSS (points)
Closes when the trade is down this many points. Works for both LONG and SHORT.
TRAILING STOP (points)
The stop follows the best price by this distance. Ex: LONG from 23150 with a 5-pt trail — price runs to 23170, the stop rides up to 23165; a pullback there closes you out, keeping most of the move. Shown live under your P&L while in a trade.
HISTORICAL DATA — for deep backtests (optional)
Export bars in NinjaTrader (Tools → Historical Data → Export) and upload here. Backtests then use YOUR data — real deep 1-minute history. Without a file, they fall back to free Yahoo data (limited to ~1 month of 1-min).
Enable any of these and they run automatically while the futures app is open — each opens ONE trade per signal and manages it with your Take-Profit / Stop / Trailing stop above. You can run more than one at a time. Test in PAPER first.
